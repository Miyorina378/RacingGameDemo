# Spur-road editing, junction geometry, and raced-layout conversion

Change add drivable spur roads, per-side dressing, terrain elevation, junction flares, raced-layout substitution, and editor controls. Raced conversion breaks old-save terrain semantics, runtime queries disagree with visible spur meshes, and editor topology changes leave index links stale. **Watch for:** **confirmed** raced old spurs drop to saved heights; **confirmed** flare zones retain invisible curb/grass/fence behavior; **confirmed** node and spur edits retarget attachments; **confirmed** converted bypasses stay open; **confirmed** spur ground selection reaches beyond or through visible surfaces.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

**Confirmed:** `raceSpurIntoPath` promotes raw spur points without resolving terrain mode and uses a different default width. Old spurs that normally follow live ground can move to stored `y` and widen when raced.

**Confirmed:** flare meshes omit curb, grass, and fence, but runtime stores one side style for the whole branch. Height, grip, and fence collision still expose those omitted features.

**Confirmed:** attachments use node and spur-array indices, but editor mutations and raced-layout filtering do not remap every reference. Normal editing can silently reconnect a branch.

**Confirmed:** the bypassed arc becomes a two-ended, unblocked spur, so the supposedly closed route remains driveable. Spur surface selection is also XZ-only and extends beyond rendered bands, breaking elevated crossings and creating invisible support.

**Confirmed:** spur left/right orientation opposes main-road orientation. Fence overshoot, multiple raced branches, negative-terrain drape, and attachment controls add further correctness gaps.

1. **High — Raced terrain/width compatibility** — **confirmed:** racing an old terrain-mode spur uses saved point heights and a wider default. Resolve terrain points and preserve the 80% width default during conversion.
2. **High — Invisible flare features** — **confirmed:** flare meshes omit curb, grass, and fence while height, grip, and collision retain them. Register and query per-sample opening state.
3. **High — Stale topology indices** — **confirmed:** node/spur insertion, deletion, filtering, and Clear All do not remap dependent attachments. Centralize mutations and update or clear every node/spur reference atomically.
4. **High — Open raced-layout bypass** — **confirmed:** the replaced arc remains connected and unblocked at both ends. Add closure geometry and matching collision at its entrances.
5. **High — Overclaimed spur ground** — **confirmed:** ground lookup returns branch height outside rendered bands and cannot distinguish stacked surfaces by Y. Reject points outside local reach and select with `yHint`.
6. **Medium — Reversed side mapping** — **confirmed:** ordinary spur rendering and raced/main-road rendering use opposite left normals. Use one orientation convention.
7. **Medium — Fence overshoot escape** — **confirmed:** lateral collision stops checking after the car crosses all surface-band flags. Check fence crossing independently, preferably swept over the frame.
8. **Medium — Only first raced branch used** — **confirmed:** editor permits several branches per layout but conversion promotes only the first. Enforce uniqueness or convert all valid branches.
9. **Medium — Negative-terrain drape clamp** — **confirmed:** Drape clamps sampled height to zero and then authors it. Preserve negative sampled heights.
10. **Medium — Broken Auto/reverse attachments** — **confirmed:** Auto clears an existing anchor and dead-end Reverse reverses points without moving the anchor. Resolve Auto immediately and disable or correctly model one-ended reversal.

<details><summary>Details</summary>

### High — Raced terrain-mode spurs change height and width

`raceSpurIntoPath` copies points into `TrackNode`s and uses `spur.width ?? config.roadWidth` at `components/modes/trackNodes.ts:104-112`. Normal spur construction uses `spur.width ?? config.roadWidth * 0.8` at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:2462-2465` and treats missing elevation mode as live terrain at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:2501-2508`; missing mode is the old-save contract at `components/engine/types.ts:175-177`. **Confirmed:** a raced old spur can move to stored heights, commonly zero, and become 25% wider. Resolve terrain-mode points from the serialized heightmap and share the normal width default.

### High — Flare visuals disagree with curb, grass, grip, and fence state

`junctionOpenAt` marks the flare at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:2562-2565`. Curb, grass, and fence builders skip it at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:2781`, `:2899`, and `:2937`, but `spurSurfaces` stores only whole-branch `leftStyle`/`rightStyle` at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:3173-3183`. `getSpurInfo` reports those styles at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:4189-4192`. **Confirmed:** the open apron has invisible curb height, grass grip, and fence collision. Carry the opening mask into runtime queries.

### High — Editor and conversion mutations corrupt attachment indices

Node insertion/deletion updates only nodes at `components/modes/PreviewMode.ts:650-670`, `components/modes/PreviewMode.ts:960-963`, and `components/Game.tsx:1206-1208`. Spur deletion and empty-spur filtering reindex arrays without rewriting `startSpurIndex`/`endSpurIndex` at `components/ui/MapEditor.tsx:1119-1122`, `components/modes/PreviewMode.ts:946-952`, and `components/Game.tsx:1191-1201`; Clear All leaves spurs behind at `components/Game.tsx:2985-2990`. **Confirmed:** attachments retarget or become invalid.

`remainingSpurs` likewise remaps only `nodeIndex`/`endNodeIndex` after removing the raced spur at `components/modes/trackNodes.ts:123-131`. **Confirmed:** branch-to-branch mouths can point at the wrong spur in a raced layout. Use shared topology helpers for editor and conversion mutations.

### High — Raced layout leaves the bypass physically open

The replaced arc is emitted with both junctions and `blocked: false` at `components/modes/trackNodes.ts:138-145`. `createSpurRoads` never creates a barrier for a two-ended spur at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:3012-3016`. **Confirmed:** the route called closed-off remains driveable and can become an unintended alternate or shortcut. Model entrance closures explicitly rather than as an open loop.

### High — Ground selection overclaims spur space

`getSpurInfo(x, z)` has no height hint and searches to `spur.maxReach + 8` at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:4129-4138`. It can return outside local asphalt/curb/grass, where `getSpurHeight` falls through to `info.baseHeight` at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:4232-4253`; `getGroundHeight` accepts that result off main asphalt at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:4260-4270`. **Confirmed:** elevated spurs create invisible support beyond their verge, and a spur crossing main tarmac cannot be selected reliably by vehicle Y. Bound the result to rendered reach and pass `yHint` through spur selection.

### Medium — Left/right flips when the spur becomes road

Main-road geometry uses `up × tangent` at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:590` and `components/modes/trackNodes.ts:265`. Spur geometry uses the opposite vector at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:2523` and applies `leftStyle` to it at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:2568-2571`; raced conversion copies those same side fields into main-road nodes at `components/modes/trackNodes.ts:104-112`. **Confirmed:** a side control moves physically across the road when the layout races that spur. Share one normal convention.

### Medium — Spur fence collision can tunnel through the rail

`enforceFenceBoundary` handles a spur only while `onAsphalt`, `onCurb`, or `onGrass` is true at `components/objects/Fence.ts:47-50`. **Confirmed:** a frame that crosses beyond the grass edge skips spur collision and may fall into unrelated main-track collision. Test the fence boundary independently of band membership, with swept crossing if possible.

### Medium — Layout conversion ignores later raced branches

The editor toggles each spur independently at `components/ui/MapEditor.tsx:1466-1499`, while conversion uses `spurs.findIndex` at `components/modes/trackNodes.ts:69-76`. **Confirmed:** only the first branch marked for a layout becomes road. Enforce one branch per layout or convert every non-overlapping match with stable remapping.

### Medium — Drape floats above negative terrain

The lower brush permits negative heights at `components/modes/terrain.ts:177-180`, but `setActiveSpurElevation` uses `Math.max(0, terrainHeight)` at `components/Game.tsx:2817-2861`; UI promises ground height at `components/ui/MapEditor.tsx:1420-1441`. **Confirmed:** Drape floats at zero in a lowered basin and locks that wrong value as authored. Save the sampled height unchanged.

### Medium — Auto and Reverse detach one-ended spurs

`Nearest (Auto)` stores `nodeIndex: undefined` at `components/ui/MapEditor.tsx:1150-1161`; existing spurs get no nearest-node recomputation. `Reverse Path` keeps an open spur’s attachment but reverses its points at `components/ui/MapEditor.tsx:1241-1256`. **Confirmed:** Auto detaches existing geometry, while Reverse reconnects the junction to the old far endpoint. Resolve Auto immediately and disable or correctly model dead-end reversal.
