# Dealer showroom cross-fades and themed 3D presentation

Change add city and brand navigation, parallel cross-fades, and themed Three.js dealer rooms. React hold transition state and send market mode plus brand color into engine. Engine rebuild GarageMode for preview changes, while GarageMode swap normal garage, tuning room, and showroom. **Watch for:** **confirmed** panel and 3D car can disagree; **confirmed** previews leak showroom GPU resources; **confirmed** ambient color and active paint do not restore; **confirmed** CATEGORIES skips reverse fade; **confirmed** purchase pulse calls wrong object.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES
