# Spur-road closures and stacked-surface selection remain unsafe

Change now preserve legacy terrain-following spurs, side-specific curb/grass/fence, authored heights, junction opening masks, topology indices, and raced-spur substitution. Task #7 add visible `blockedStart`/`blockedEnd` walls for bypassed live-road arcs while leaving ordinary loops and legacy dead ends on old behavior. Most earlier findings are closed, but closure collision points to wrong safe side and stacked-road selection still chooses wrong deck. Raced-layout editor state has reachable control/topology regressions, and Reverse Path does not remap endpoint closure meaning. **Watch for:** **confirmed, High** both generated entrance barriers move live-road car into closed bypass; **confirmed, High** support and slope choose stacked surfaces without full Y awareness; **confirmed, Medium** barriers collide across elevations; **confirmed, Medium** spur-only layouts cannot be previewed/test-driven; **confirmed, Medium** endpoint deletion leaves ghost raced layouts; **confirmed, Low** reversing explicit closures moves them to opposite physical ends.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Generated bypass get two visible walls and two collision records, but both normals point into bypass and solver resolves onto positive-normal side. Live-road approach gets translated past wall instead of kept outside. Ordinary two-ended loops have no closure flags, and old unattached dead ends still use one legacy GT barrier, so regression is scoped to new two-mouth closures.

Road and spur searches accept `yHint`, but final main-versus-spur arbitration still uses XZ distance; slope path drops hint entirely. Barrier solver also discards vertical offset, so wall on one deck affects car on another.

Raced spur can publish Long/Short without tagged track node, while editor selector only checks node tags. Removing raced spur endpoint retains assignment, advertises variation converter cannot use, and disables control needed to clear it.

Persistence and editor-to-runtime copies carry new fields, but Reverse Path swaps geometry and attachments without swapping `blockedStart`/`blockedEnd`.

Legacy block remains gated by unattached end plus `spur.blocked ?? true` at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:3090-3096`; ordinary loop and old dead-end behavior remain separate.

### High — Y-aware searches still choose stacked surface by XZ

`getSpurInfo` scores candidates with `yHint` at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:4257-4263`, and `getTrackInfo` also includes Y. `getGroundHeight` then lets spur win only off main tarmac or when spur XZ distance is strictly smaller at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:4331-4344`. **Confirmed:** crossing probe with main deck y=0 and spur deck y≈20 returned spur height 19.95 for hint 20, but `getGroundHeight(0, 0, 20)` returned lower deck at -0.05 because centerline distances tied.

`getSlopeHeight` accepts only X/Z and queries without hint at `components/modes/BaseMode.ts:4163-4173`; vehicle calls it without car Y at `components/objects/Vehicle.ts:2516-2525`. **Confirmed:** overlapping-road probe selected upper deck with hint 25 but slope selected lower deck at 0, feeding wrong pitch, roll, and gravity.

### Medium — Endpoint barriers collide across elevation levels

Barrier carries center and height, but collision sets `toCar.y = 0` and tests only XZ extents at `components/objects/Fence.ts:14-22`; `barrier.height` is unused. **Confirmed:** car directly above or below closure enters same collision branch as car on wall deck, allowing authored overpass to hit invisible obstacle.
