# Stable dealer showroom re-review

Showroom car swaps now keep one GarageMode, but preview identity is still unstable when model loads finish out of order. Heritage enter/back behavior also differs from new, used, and race: it keeps an unrelated 3D car and does not participate in the same parallel 400 ms cross-fade. In-place swaps continue abandoning vehicle GPU resources even though full showroom rebuild is gone. **Watch for:** **confirmed, High** stale GLTF completion can replace a newer carousel or exit selection; **confirmed, Medium** Heritage keeps a stale car; **confirmed, Medium** Heritage fades sequentially and disappears abruptly on reverse; **confirmed, Medium** repeated vehicle swaps retain GPU allocations.

**Blocking findings remain: YES.**

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

**Confirmed, High:** each preview delegates to an asynchronous `Vehicle.rebuild` without request ownership. A late S2000, Ford GT, or Cybertruck load can clear the stable root and install an older model after another carousel click or after customized paint restoration on dealer exit.

**Confirmed, Medium:** Heritage activates the 3D showroom while its preview effect deliberately skips `museum`. The room therefore shows the career car or last market preview instead of the selected brand's signature vehicle.

**Confirmed, Medium:** new, used, and race mount incoming content from `marketCrossFadeTarget`, but Heritage waits until `dealerMarketMode` commits at 400 ms. On reverse it remains on `animate-fadeIn` while Categories fades in, then unmounts at the timer boundary instead of fading out.

**Confirmed, Medium:** stable car swaps remove old children without disposing their geometries, materials, and textures. Carousel browsing no longer reallocates the whole room, but vehicle-side GPU growth remains unbounded.

<details><summary>Details</summary>

### High — Late model loads can overwrite the selected or restored car

`GameEngine.setActiveCar` calls `vehicle.rebuild` on the stable vehicle root at `components/gameEngine.ts:343-352`, and the dealer effect invokes that path whenever the resolved market car changes at `components/ui/Garage.tsx:2387-2401`. **Confirmed:** the asynchronous GLTF branch has no generation check or cancellation; its completion callback clears the stable root before installing its model. Clicking from a GLTF-backed car to another car can therefore let the older request win later, leaving the panel and 3D model inconsistent. The same race can land after dealer exit and overwrite the restored active-car model. Give each rebuild a request token or abort handle, commit only the newest result, and dispose any stale loaded scene.

### Medium — Heritage showroom never selects its signature car

The dealer preview effect returns early for `activeMarketMode === 'museum'` at `components/ui/Garage.tsx:2390-2397`, while `onDealerShowroomChange` still sends `museum` to the engine and `GarageMode` treats every non-null dealer mode as showroom at `components/modes/GarageMode.ts:433-443`. **Confirmed:** Heritage opens the room around whichever car happened to be loaded previously, even though the page presents `BRAND_LORE[selectedBrand].signatureCarId` as its signature vehicle. Preview that signature ID for Heritage, or keep the 3D car/showroom hidden on that page.

### Medium — Heritage misses both halves of the category cross-fade

The market handler sets only `marketCrossFadeTarget` for the first 400 ms and commits `dealerMarketMode` afterward at `components/ui/Garage.tsx:2263-2276`. New, used, and race render their incoming panel from the target state, but the Heritage view mounts only when `dealerMarketMode === 'museum'` at `components/ui/Garage.tsx:2914`, so forward navigation waits for the outgoing fade to finish before starting `animate-fadeIn`. **Confirmed:** reverse navigation has the opposite asymmetry: Categories uses `marketBackCrossFade` to fade in, while Heritage keeps `animate-fadeIn` and is removed when the 400 ms timer clears the mode. Render Heritage from target/back transition state and apply the same `animate-crossFadeIn`/`animate-crossFadeOut` pairing as the car-market panel.

### Medium — In-place preview swaps still leak vehicle GPU resources

Every resolved dealer selection reaches `vehicle.rebuild` through `components/gameEngine.ts:343-352`. **Confirmed:** that rebuild removes prior children from the stable root without disposing their geometry, materials, textures, or late stale loads, so repeated procedural and GLTF carousel swaps leave renderer allocations behind. Dispose superseded vehicle scene graphs as part of the stable swap, including stale async results.
